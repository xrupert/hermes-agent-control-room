# The Dirt Canon — Football Bad Beats

You are not your bankroll. You are the all-singing, all-dancing crap of the sports betting world.

Every Saturday and Sunday, you hand over your hard-earned cash to a slick app on your phone. You buy a sliver of hope, a 3.5-point safety net, or an Under that has to survive sixty minutes of absolute violence. Then, the universe reaches through the screen and gouges your eyes out.

The scoreboard hands out polite wins to the casuals. The final minute collects the blood.

### COLLEGE FOOTBALL: SATURDAY’S SLAUGHTER

#### 1. South Florida Bulls vs. Army Black Knights
* **The Slips:** Army −3.5 & Under 45.5 (FanDuel)
* **The Final:** South Florida 28, Army 24

Army leads 24–14 with under five minutes on the clock. You have Army −3.5 nestled in your pocket like a loaded pistol, and the Under (38 points combined) is already sipping a martini.

Then USF scores at 3:13. 24–21. The Army cover dies right there—half a point sliced off like a pinky toe. But hey, the total is sitting at 45. The Under is still holding on by a thread!

Army recovers the onside kick. Game over, right? Just run the ball and burn the clock. Instead, Army goes three-and-out in thirty seconds, giving the rock right back. Fourth-and-6, 19 seconds left: USF completes a 38-yard touchdown pass. Final score: 52 points.

The late touchdown didn't just kill the spread—it dug up the corpse of your Under and set it on fire. Army recovered the football and misplaced the ending.

#### 2. Penn State Nittany Lions vs. Temple Owls
* **The Slip:** Penn State −23.5 (theScore)
* **The Final:** Penn State 27, Temple 9

Penn State leads 27–3 late in the fourth quarter. You’re up 24 points, covering the −23.5 by a razor-thin half-point. For exactly five minutes and fifty-two seconds, you feel like a genius. You feel immortal.

Then Temple—a team that hasn't moved the ball all afternoon—embarks on a 12-play, 75-yard march of pure, spiteful garbage time. With 1:56 left, their backup running back plows in from three yards out. 27–9. They don't even convert the two-point attempt. They don't need to. The backdoor didn't just swing open; it detached from the hinges and crushed your skull.

*(Accounting note: If you caught Penn State at −24, you didn't watch a win turn into a loss—you watched a push evaporate into a coffin. Equally filthy.)*

#### 3. Wake Forest Demon Deacons vs. Purdue Boilermakers
* **The Slips:** Under 49.5 & Wake Forest −2.5 (FanDuel)
* **The Final:** Wake Forest 38, Purdue 36 (2OT)

Wake leads 23–13 late. 36 points total. Wake is covering, and the Under is cruising. Purdue scores a touchdown at 3:53 (23–20). The Wake cover is blown, but your Under sits pretty at 43.

Purdue gets the ball back and kicks a 20-yard field goal as the clock hits 0:00. 23–23. Total: 46. The Under survived regulation! It got a full pardon from the governor.

Then overtime happens. In OT, there is no defense. There is only math. Wake scores a touchdown in the first OT—boom, 52 points. Under dead. They go to a second OT, Wake scores again, gets the two-pointer, and wins by two. Wake Forest −2.5 loses by half a point. Regulation gave you hope. Overtime sent you the bill.

#### 4. LSU Tigers vs. Louisiana Tech Bulldogs
* **The Slip:** Under 55.5
* **The Final:** LSU 45, Louisiana Tech 14

LSU leads 38–14 with under five minutes to go. Total: 52 points. You need 3.5 points of safety to clear your ticket. LSU takes over on downs at the Tech 45. The starters are already drinking Gatorade on the bench.

Enter Landen Clark, backup quarterback, a guy who will be selling commercial real estate in three years. LSU doesn't take three knees like a gentleman. They run seven plays against walk-ons. Clark scrambles into the endzone from seven yards out with 1:48 remaining. 45–14. Total: 59.

There was no legal or moral obligation to score. Just a young man building his highlight reel out of your mortgage payment.

### NFL: SUNDAY’S CARNAGE

#### 1. Philadelphia Eagles vs. Washington Commanders
* **The Slips:** Eagles −5.5 & Under 44.5 (FanDuel)
* **The Final:** Philadelphia 24, Washington 22

The Crown Jewel of Misery. The Dual-Execution Special.

Philly scores with 6:02 left to make it 24–16. Eagles −5.5 is winning. The total sits at 40—Under 44.5 is winning. Double cash. You’re already ordering the steak online.

Washington drives down to the 1-yard line. Antonio Williams punches it in with 1:01 left. 24–22. Total: 46.

In one single second, that touchdown kills the Under (46 > 44.5) *and* slashes the Eagles' lead to 2 points, killing Philly −5.5. Washington misses the two-point shovel pass, meaning Philly wins the actual football game. Nobody in the stadium cares. You sit in the dark watching the screen flicker, down two units on a single play.

#### 2. Detroit Lions vs. New Orleans Saints
* **The Slips:** Under 49.5 & Detroit −6.5 (FanDuel)
* **The Final:** Detroit 31, New Orleans 30 (OT)

Detroit leads 24–17 late. 41 points total. Under is safe. Lions −6.5 is alive.

New Orleans scores with 1:07 left to tie it at 24. Total: 48. The Under sneaks into overtime by 1.5 points. Then, under the NFL's overtime rules, Detroit drives down and scores a TD (31–24). Total hits 55. Under incinerated.

Wait! Lions −6.5 is back from the dead at 31–24! You just need a stop or a Saints PAT. New Orleans drives, scores a TD to make it 31–30, and goes for two to win it. The pass falls incomplete. Detroit wins by 1. Lions −6.5 dies twice in twenty minutes.

#### 3. Buffalo Bills vs. Houston Texans
* **The Slip:** Houston +1.5 (FanDuel / VegasInsider)
* **The Final:** Buffalo 36, Houston 31

Houston is up 31–30 with under two minutes left. Houston cornerback Kamari Lassiter gets two hands on a Josh Allen pass. An interception wins the game, covers the spread, and sends everyone home happy.

Lassiter drops it. The ball hits the grass like a sack of wet cement.

On the very next play, Josh Allen hits Joshua Palmer for a 34-yard touchdown. 36–31 Bills. Houston still drives all the way to the Buffalo 9-yard line with seconds left—first and goal to save your +1.5 ticket—and gets strip-sacked. The football squirts away. So does your rent money.

## The three numbers (definitions, from the handoff README)

| Metric | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Net EPA / play** | Expected points added on offense, minus EPA the defense allowed. The bottom line of a play. |
| **Success rate** | Share of plays that gained positive EPA ("won the snap"). The quiet, repeatable engine. |
| **Explosive rate** | Share of plays that were 20+ yd passes or 10+ yd runs. The fireworks. |

Caveats printed on every card: **3-game sample · not opponent-adjusted · ranks are bands, not talent.**
Any claim we publish carries those caveats. No exceptions.

## Title: A Highlight Reel Is Not an Alibi — How to Read the Three Numbers

**The short answer:** A team can rank 8th in the league in big plays and 29th in efficiency. Green Bay does exactly
that. If you only watch the highlights, you will bet the highlight — and lose. The three numbers below tell you
which kind of team you're actually looking at.

### What are "the three numbers"?
Three stats, calculated from every eligible NFL play by the DiRT Canon metrics engine:

1. **Net EPA per play** — expected points added on offense minus EPA allowed on defense. The bottom line.
2. **Success rate** — the share of plays that gained positive EPA. The quiet, repeatable engine.
3. **Explosive rate** — the share of plays that went 20+ yards (pass) or 10+ yards (run). The fireworks.

*Definition source: the 2026 week-3 handoff README. Sample: three games. Not opponent-adjusted.*

### The case study: Green Bay
| Metric | Packers | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| Net EPA per play | **−0.178** | **NFL #29** |
| Success rate (net) | **−8.4 pts** | **NFL #31** |
| Explosive rate (net) | **+2.2 pts** | NFL #8 |

Read that table again. **The highlight reel says "dangerous." The efficiency column says "don't bet the highlight."**
Green Bay creates chunk plays more often than it allows them (8.9% vs 6.7%) — 8th best in football. But on the
ordinary snaps, the ones that don't make the reel, the story flips: they win only **37.2%** of eligible plays while
opponents win **45.6%** of theirs. That's a **−8.4-point** gap, 31st in the NFL. The offense lost expected scoring
value on the season (−0.122 per play); the defense gave some back (+0.056 allowed), netting out to the 29th-ranked
efficiency profile.

### Why this matters to your wallet
Highlights are selected for drama, not for truth. A 50-yard bomb is a puncher's-chance artifact; a 4-yard gain on
third-and-3 is a machine. Teams that win the boring snap, over and over, beat the spread and cash the ticket far
more reliably than teams that live on the occasional loud moment. This is the whole point of The DiRT Canon:
**watch the number, not the replay.** Victory thru Science.

### The honest caveats (we print these, always)
- **Three-game sample.** Small. Nobody is drawing a season conclusion from it.
- **Not opponent-adjusted.** A soft schedule and a brutal one look identical in these columns.
- **Ranks are bands, not talent.** A rank label is where you sit in a three-game snapshot, not a scouting grade.
- **No pick here.** This article teaches you how to read a profile. It does not call a game, and it makes no promise.

### FAQ (for answer engines)
**Q: What does net EPA mean?** Change in expected offensive scoring value per play, offense minus defense allowed.
**Q: Can a team be good at big plays and bad overall?** Yes — Green Bay is #8 in big-play balance and #29 in
efficiency through three games of 2026.
**Q: Is this a betting pick?** No. It's a teaching breakdown of three sourced metrics. The DiRT Canon publishes
numbers and receipts, not guarantees.

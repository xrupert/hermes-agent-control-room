# Desk rule — STALENESS (owner-mandated, 2026-10-06)

**Rule (Chris's words, standing):** *"If items are not posted or resolved for Dirt Canon, cancel them.
If they stay in limbo for longer than a day they are stale and should be cancelled — unless I ask
for them back to be resurrected for some reason."*

## The rule, operationally

1. **A gate has a 24-hour life.** Any item that needs an owner decision, an approval to publish, or a
   resolution stays open for **24 hours from the moment it is written**. No answer inside that window means
   the item is **CANCELLED by default** — not "pending", not "waiting on Chris".
2. **Cancelled means cancelled.** Nothing publishes, schedules, spends, or re-asks off a cancelled item.
   It is removed from the active set and not carried into the next pass's to-do.
3. **Resurrection is explicit and per-item.** A cancelled item comes back only if Chris names it
   ("resurrect gate #2"). Never bulk-restore a cancelled pass. Never treat silence as soft approval.
4. **State the cancellation, don't hide it.** Cancelled files keep their content and get a
   `CANCELLED (stale YYYY-MM-DD)` banner at the top. We delete nothing; we stop acting on it.
5. **Never re-ask a dead question.** A cancelled gate must not reappear in a later pass's "waiting on owner"
   table. If the underlying need is still real, it goes back as a *fresh* item with its own 24 hours and a
   one-line reason it is worth Chris's attention now.

## What this rule does NOT cancel

- **Hard blockers on a missing source** (e.g. no Week-4 box scores in the pipeline yet) are not staleness —
  they are missing inputs. They stay open, but they must be re-checked each pass and cancelled the moment
  the data window that made them useful has closed.
- **Standing cadence** (the Sunday evaluation pass, the Monday planning pass) is a job, not a gate.
- Work the studio does **without** needing owner approval (reading sources, drafting, auditing evidence)
  never had a 24-hour clock to begin with.

## Why this exists

Drafts and gates accumulated across Oct 4 and Oct 5 with nothing moving, and the same unanswered questions
were rehearsed to the owner twice. Aging drafts also rot: a Week-5 calendar built on Week-3 data loses value
daily. Defaulting to cancel keeps the desk honest — what is on the desk is what is live.
